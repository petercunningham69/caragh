Our Club

One of Kildare’s oldest clubs

Caragh GFC plays at Saint Farnan’s Park in Prosperous, and shares a parish with Raheens. By the end of the 1910s Caragh was a real force, winning senior titles in 1918, 1919 and 1926. Larry Stanley captained the 1918 side and went on to win Leinster and All-Ireland honours with Kildare.

The 1960s and 70s were lean years, but the club kept going. The 1990s brought the Junior title and Jack Higgins Cup in 1993 and the Intermediate title in 1995, followed by a senior semi-final in 1997. In 2024 the senior men won Kildare and then Leinster.
